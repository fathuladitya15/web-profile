About Me

I am a Fullstack Developer with over 5 years of experience building web-based and enterprise applications. My expertise includes developing Human Resource Management (HRM) systems, payroll and attendance platforms, RESTful APIs, and cloud-based solutions using Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, and AWS. Throughout my career, I have been involved in the full software development lifecycle, from system analysis and database design to deployment and maintenance. I have contributed to various business-critical applications, including Smart HRM, E-RUPS, payroll management systems, attendance tracking platforms, and mobile application backends. In addition to web development, I have experience integrating hardware devices with software solutions, including Bluetooth-enabled digital weighing systems using Python for real-time data processing and web integration. I am passionate about building scalable, reliable, and user-focused applications that solve real business challenges.
